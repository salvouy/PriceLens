# PriceLens

PriceLens uses your phone camera to read product prices and display an estimated conversion in a currency you choose.

- Available in Spanish, English, and Portuguese.
- Price recognition runs on the device.
- Conversions use daily reference exchange rates and may differ from rates or fees at checkout.
